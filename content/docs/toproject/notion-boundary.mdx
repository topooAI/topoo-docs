## Purpose
This document defines the role of Notion inside the `toProject` architecture.

## Block Position
Inside the `toProject` board, this page belongs to the `Boundary & Version Strategy` block.

## Core Position
Notion is the `thinking and loose knowledge surface`.

Notion owns:
- notes
- meeting context
- strategy drafts
- wiki pages
- open thinking
- soft knowledge capture

Notion does not own:
- strong project execution state
- run history
- reviewable source version
- runtime governance
- deploy truth

## Why Notion Still Matters
Notion remains useful because not every idea should become structured project data immediately.

Teams need a place for:
- messy thinking
- early framing
- context gathering
- long-form internal explanation

That is where Notion is strong.

But that same looseness becomes a liability when execution complexity rises.

Without stronger purpose management and structured memory:
- intent fragments across pages
- memory becomes diffuse
- agents must read too much loose context
- token cost rises
- execution focus weakens

This is why Notion should remain the loose knowledge layer, while `toProject` becomes the purpose-managed execution layer.

## Boundary
Notion should feed `toProject`.

It should not replace `toProject`.

When a thing becomes execution-critical, it must leave the loose knowledge layer and enter the project control layer.
